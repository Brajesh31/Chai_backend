# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes participation from people with different backgrounds and experience. Everyone taking part in project spaces is expected to help keep them respectful, useful, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code; do not attack or demean people.
- Accept good-faith corrections and focus on the project's shared goals.
- Give useful context when reporting a problem and respect other contributors' time.
- Keep private information private and do not publish credentials or personal data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, deliberate disruption, sexualized attention without consent, doxxing, and abusive or insulting language are not acceptable. This also applies to persistent unwanted contact and retaliation against someone who raises a concern.

## Maintainer response

Project maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to protect contributors and keep work productive. Responses should be proportionate to the conduct and may include a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the contact method on the `@brajesh31` GitHub profile. Include links or relevant context, but avoid sharing sensitive personal information unnecessarily. Do not report security vulnerabilities in public; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). If the concern involves the maintainer, use GitHub's platform reporting tools.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other spaces when someone is representing or working on this project.
